Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Mauzobora ("Mauzobora", "we", "us" or "our") handles information when you visit or use the Mauzobora shop-management service. It applies to visitors, registered users and people who contact our support team.

In short: We use account and shop information to provide the service, keep it working, support users, process subscriptions and protect accounts. We do not sell personal information.

1. Information we handle

  • Account information: your email address, authentication identifiers supplied by Firebase or Google, account creation details and business name or profile information you provide.
  • Shop information: products, stock, customers, sales, expenses, receipts, settings and other business records that you enter or create in Mauzobora.
  • Subscription and payment information: selected plan, subscription status, payment references and transaction status. When you pay, the payment provider may collect and process information such as your mobile-money number and payment details under its own terms and privacy policy. We do not ask you to send passwords, PINs or one-time payment codes to our support team.
  • Technical and usage information: basic device, browser, diagnostic and service-usage information that may be processed by Firebase services and, where enabled, Firebase Analytics.
  • Support communications: the phone number, message and other information you choose to share when contacting us.
  • Voice input: if you choose to use voice features, your browser's speech-recognition service may process audio or a transcript to provide the requested feature. Processing is subject to the browser and speech-service provider's own practices.

2. How we use information

We use information to create and authenticate accounts; operate your shop dashboard; save and synchronize your business records; show plan and trial status; request and verify subscription payments; provide customer support; troubleshoot errors; maintain security; prevent misuse; and improve the reliability of Mauzobora.

3. Services that help us operate

Mauzobora uses third-party infrastructure and service providers, including Firebase services for authentication, database hosting and related app functions, Google sign-in when you choose it, and a payment provider for subscription transactions. These providers process information as needed to deliver their services and under their own terms and privacy documentation. We do not control their independent privacy practices.

4. Storage, security and retention

Shop records are associated with your account and stored using the application's cloud database. Some information may also be cached on your device to support app performance and offline behavior. We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information, but no internet service or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

We keep information for as long as reasonably needed to provide the service, maintain account and transaction records, resolve disputes, meet legal obligations and protect the service. Backup copies or records held by service providers may remain for a limited period after an account-related request.

5. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share the minimum information reasonably needed with infrastructure, authentication and payment providers; when required by law or valid legal process; to protect users, the service or our rights; or when you direct us to share information as part of using a feature.

6. Your choices and requests

You are responsible for keeping your sign-in credentials secure and for the accuracy of the shop information you enter. You may contact us to ask about access, correction or deletion of information associated with your account. We may need to verify that you control the account and may retain some records where required for legitimate operational, security or legal reasons.

7. Children

Mauzobora is a business-management service and is not designed for children. Do not create an account or submit personal information if you are not legally able to use the service in your location.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the service or applicable requirements change. We will publish the revised version on this page and update the date above. Continued use after an update means you have had an opportunity to review the revised policy.
