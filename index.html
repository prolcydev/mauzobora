06FAQ

Common questions

Need more help? Call or WhatsApp +255 621 658 007.

Can I create an account for free?

Yes. Registration and dashboard preview are free. You can explore the dashboard, but you need to activate a paid plan before adding or changing business records, making sales or using the shop tools.

When do I need to pay?

Only when you are ready to use Mauzobora. Choose Basic, Premium or Ultra and complete the mobile-money payment. Your tools unlock automatically after payment is confirmed.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. Mauzobora works on phones, tablets and computers.

Is my business data private?

Your records are private and visible only to you.

How do I get help?

Call or WhatsApp our support team on +255 621 658 007.
